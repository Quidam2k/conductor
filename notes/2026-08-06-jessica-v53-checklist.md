1. Open **https://quidam2k.github.io/conductor/** in Safari.
2. Footer must say **build v53** — if not, pull down to refresh once.
3. Tap **Manage Packs**. If "Demo: The Stillness" isn't listed:
   download **https://quidam2k.github.io/conductor/packs/demo-the-stillness.zip**
