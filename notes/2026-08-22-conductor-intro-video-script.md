# Conductor — Intro Video Script (written for generative video)

**Pipeline #1018 / assignment #3165. Draft 1 — 2026-08-22.**
Target length **~75 s**. Tone: **energetic but warm** (it has to hold both "protest"
and "art / group meditation"). Human anchor appears only at the **hook** and the
**CTA**; everything between is visuals that SHOW the architecture, with voice-over.

> **Status:** copy/message pass is Todd's. Scene visuals are promptable as written;
> tune casting, locations, and the exact anchor lines to taste before generation.

---

## What the MiniMax guide implies for shot structure

Researched the current MiniMax **H3 (Hailuo 3.0)** guides (fal / getimg / cdance,
Aug 2026). Points that shape this script:

- **H3 is omni-modal with NATIVE audio** — picture and stereo sound are generated
  together in one pass. Dialogue, ambience, and effects are baked into each clip,
  not layered afterward.
- **Clips run ~5–15 s.** So the piece is authored as a **sequence of short scenes**,
  each independently promptable — not one long narration track. Every scene below is
  sized to one H3 generation.
- **Prompt like production paperwork:** a timed shot description (subject, motion,
  camera), then the sound directed as deliberately as the picture. Give each on-screen
  element an explicit job.
- **Direct the audio every scene** — "silence is never the default"; an unspecified
  soundtrack is still a generated one. So each scene carries an explicit AUDIO line.
- **Dialogue in its own block; describe the voice outside it.** Use a **stable speaker
  id** ("ANCHOR") wherever a person talks so the same voice recurs. Keep spoken words
  short — one or two lines per clip.
- **Continuity is per-clip.** For the anchor and any recurring crowd look, reuse an
  identical description (or an image/omni-reference) across scenes so casting doesn't
  drift.

Production note: generate each scene as its own H3 clip, then cut together. Where a
scene is pure visual + VO, let H3 make the ambient bed and lay the VO over it in the
edit (or prompt the VO as off-screen narration — the guide supports both).

## SCENE 1 — Cold hook (0:00–0:08) · anchor + reveal

**VISUAL PROMPT:** A busy sunlit plaza, ordinary foot traffic. A young woman
(ANCHOR, casual jacket, phone in hand) stands center, glances at her screen, then
looks up. Hard cut to a wide overhead shot: ~40 strangers scattered across the plaza
**all freeze in the exact same instant** — mid-step, mid-laugh. Pigeons scatter. Camera
slowly pushes in on the stillness. Natural daylight, documentary realism.

**AUDIO:** Plaza ambience — footsteps, chatter — that **snaps to near-silence** on the
freeze; a single soft low tone marks the instant. No music yet.

**DIALOGUE (ANCHOR, warm, direct to camera, just before the freeze):**
> "Watch what a whole crowd can do — with nothing but the phones already in their pockets."

---

## SCENE 2 — The idea (0:08–0:18) · VO over montage

**VISUAL PROMPT:** Fast, rhythmic montage of three coordinated moments, ~2 s each:
(a) a line of umbrellas opening in sequence down a rainy street; (b) a dark field of
faces lit at once by phone flashlights clicking on; (c) a plaza of people raising signs
in unison. Snappy match-cuts on motion.

**AUDIO:** A warm pulse of music enters and starts to build. Each cut lands on a soft
percussive hit.

**VO (off-screen narrator, warm):**
> "Conductor coordinates real-world moments — performances, protests, art — so everyone
> moves on the same beat. No leader out front. No app to download."

---

## SCENE 3 — Record player + LP metaphor (0:18–0:30) · the architecture, shown

**VISUAL PROMPT:** Clean, tactile motion-graphic on a dark studio backdrop. A single
glowing file icon labeled **conductor.html** spins up like a vinyl record on a turntable
("the record player"). A second small glowing chip labeled with a QR code drops onto it
like an LP ("the event"). As the needle touches down, faint timed cue-marks ripple
outward in a circle. Text, minimal, appears and fades: **"one file • no server • no
account."** Elegant, Apple-keynote-clean.

**AUDIO:** A satisfying mechanical "click" as the LP lands; a soft vinyl-warmth swell.
Music holds under.

**VO:**
> "The whole app is one file — the record player. Your event is just data — the record.
> No server ever sees your plan; it lives in a link."

---

## SCENE 4 — Viral transfer / QR Beam (0:30–0:42) · shown, device-to-device

**VISUAL PROMPT:** Two phones held side by side on a plaza bench, close macro shot. The
left phone screen shows an **animated QR code cycling through frames**; the right phone's
camera is held up to it and a **progress bar fills**. The right phone then flips to an
event-ready screen. Pull back: the second person turns and holds their phone up to a
**third** person's phone — the same transfer ripples outward, person to person. No wifi
symbols anywhere.

**AUDIO:** Soft electronic "transfer" shimmer as the bar fills; a bright confirmation
chime when it completes, repeating as it passes down the line.

**VO:**
> "Two phones next to each other pass everything across — even with the network jammed.
> And whoever catches it can pass it on."

---

## SCENE 5 — Offline / phone in pocket (0:42–0:52) · shown

**VISUAL PROMPT:** A person slides their **locked** phone into a jacket pocket and looks
up, hands free, eyes on the crowd. Cut to a tight shot of the pocket; faint audio-wave
lines pulse out of it in rhythm. Cut to their face reacting — a small nod — exactly as a
cue lands. Golden-hour light, calm and human.

**AUDIO:** Muffled-from-pocket **countdown beeps then a trigger beep**, followed by a
soft spoken cue in a warm recorded voice ("...raise them now"). Ambient crowd under it.

**VO:**
> "Lock it, pocket it. The cues keep playing — beeps and real recorded voices — so your
> eyes stay on the moment, not the screen."

---

## SCENE 6 — Resource packs + emergent harmony (0:52–1:02) · shown

**VISUAL PROMPT:** Split-screen grid of six phones, each a different person. A cue fires;
each phone emits a slightly different sung note (visualized as colored sound-rings). The
rings overlap and bloom together into one chord across the grid. Warm, concert-like.

**AUDIO:** Six individual voices/notes that **layer into a single rich chord** — the
"emergent harmony." Music opens up underneath.

**VO:**
> "Add a resource pack and robot speech becomes real voices — even music. With a crowd,
> everyone's single note becomes one sound."

---

## SCENE 7 — Possibilities montage (1:02–1:10) · fast, wide

**VISUAL PROMPT:** Rapid ~1.5 s cuts, escalating: a stadium doing a **light wave** across
the stands at night; a silent protest where hundreds kneel at once; a gallery crowd in a
slow synchronized gesture; a park group in a shared breathing meditation. End on the
widest, most striking aerial.

**AUDIO:** Music reaches its peak; a crowd "whoa" swells under the stadium wave, then
drops to a held, hopeful chord on the final aerial.

**VO:**
> "A performance. A protest. A shared breath. Same tool — it just puts everyone on the
> same beat."

---

## SCENE 8 — CTA (1:10–1:15) · anchor, direct to camera

**VISUAL PROMPT:** Back to ANCHOR from Scene 1 (identical look, for continuity), now on a
quiet street at dusk, holding up her phone showing the Conductor home screen. She smiles
at the camera. Clean end-card fades in beside her: **"Conductor — one file. Open it, make
something happen."** with the project URL underneath.

**AUDIO:** Music resolves warmly. One final soft cue-beep as the end-card lands.

**DIALOGUE (ANCHOR):**
> "It's free, it's one file, and it works offline. Open it — and make something happen."

## Casting / continuity cheatsheet (for prompting)

- **ANCHOR** (Scenes 1 & 8): same person, wardrobe, and framing both times — carry an
  omni-reference image if the model supports it, else reuse the identical description.
- **Crowd look** (Scenes 1, 4, 7): consistent daylight documentary grade so the freeze,
  the beam relay, and the montage read as one world.
- **Cue sound signature:** the ascending countdown beeps + single trigger beep recur in
  Scenes 5 and 8 — keep them identical so the "sound of Conductor" is learned by the end.
- **Text on screen:** only three text moments (Scene 3 tagline, Scene 8 end-card, URL).
  Keep everything else visual.
