# Code of Conduct

Conductor exists to help people do things together peacefully. We hold the project's own spaces to the same standard.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with newcomers and non-technical testers.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, slurs, sexualized content, threats, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Don't use Conductor's spaces to organize harm to people.

**Reporting:** if something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/Quidam2k/conductor/security/advisories/new). Mark it "conduct" in the title. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content and block participants who break these rules.
